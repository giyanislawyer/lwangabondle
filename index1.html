Pink Envelope That Did Not Make Cut

Wrote something every month. Some were lost.

Eagerly I wait like a hound yearning for the return of its owner. Time seems to stand still in this moment, but as I check my watch, it rushes steadily onward. Autumn leaves have fallen, the dead have been mourned, my noir skin crackles from the thirst of touch, still, my parched lips echo your name. Eagerly, I still wait to be in your blissful embrace, for your laughter to serenade me again. Oh, how this grief has befallen me, as I stand in this without your presence.
Oh, for I am but an orange moon, cursed to radiate the inferno candlelight fire of my love for you. This light is merely a reflection, and not the love in its entirety. I refuse the concept of being a stingy, limited vessel, but more of a guardian angel. For the fire that burns results in a death for the both of us. It burns vigorously and without bounds.
As the sun rises, its light gleams through the crack of your curtain, spilling across your caramel skin, innocence in its purest form, a sight of ethereal beauty. Your eyes open, your lashes flutter a solemn prayer only God can hear. And in that stillness, my heart lifts a hymn to heaven.
Occasionally, a poet. Part-time, a painter. I could show you my love in brushstrokes on canvas, or carve it into verse— describe our love as what i feel and see
